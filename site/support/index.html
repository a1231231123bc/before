Questions, a bug, or an idea? Contact Frameago’s developer, Pavel Kurmachev.

pavelkurmachev@gmail.com

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS version, Frameago version (in About), and what happened. A screenshot can help, but please remove private information first. Don’t send passwords or your photo library.

How do I start?

Tap Take photo or Library at the bottom of the home screen. Your first photo starts a story automatically. Open the story to add another moment later. Titles, dates, notes, and categories can be edited when needed.

How do I compare photos?

Open a story with at least two moments and choose Compare photos. Pick the two moments, then drag the slider. The included sample story lets you try this before adding your own photos; its two different plants are demonstration images.

How do I save or share a video?

On the comparison screen, choose Create 6-second video. Frameago makes a six-second, 1080 × 1920 MP4 with an animated slider. Keep the app open while it prepares. Preview it, then save it to Photos or use Share video. You can also import the saved video from Photos into Instagram or another editor.

What if a permission is denied?

Camera access is only needed to take a photo. You can still use the system photo picker. Saving a video asks for permission to add to Photos; you can also use the share sheet. To change a denied permission, open your device’s Settings, find Frameago, and review its permissions.

Where is my journal stored?

On your device. Frameago has no account, cloud sync, or developer-operated backup. Device backups depend on your Apple settings. Removing Frameago can remove its journal; exported images and videos are keepsakes, not a complete backup that can be restored into the app.

How do I delete a photo or story?

Open the moment or story menu and choose Delete, then confirm. This removes the app’s local copy. Original photos and exports already saved to Photos or sent to other apps are separate copies; delete those in their respective apps if needed.

Is Frameago free?

Yes. The initial release has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertisements.
