Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Frameago 1.0

Frameago stores your journal on your device. The app does not send your photos, notes, journal entries, identifiers, or usage activity to the developer. There are no accounts, advertisements, tracking, analytics SDKs, or cloud sync.

Who is responsible?

Frameago is developed by Pavel Kurmachev. For privacy questions or support, contact pavelkurmachev@gmail.com.

Your journal and photos

Photos you take or select, story titles, categories, dates, and notes are processed and stored locally in the app’s storage. Photo comparisons and videos are generated on your device. The developer cannot access your journal.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested when you choose to take a photo.
  • Selected photos: the system photo picker lets you choose the images to import.
  • Add to Photos: requested when you save an exported video to your photo library.

You can decline permissions or change them in your device’s Settings. Features that depend on a declined permission may be unavailable. Frameago does not request microphone access or track your location.

Sharing and external services

Exporting does not automatically publish anything. When you save or share a comparison, the chosen file is passed to Photos or the app you select. Those services handle it under their own policies. External links open only when you choose them; their websites have their own privacy practices.

Frameago does not sell personal data or use data for advertising. No third-party SDK receives your journal or usage analytics from the app.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Your journal remains on your device until you delete its photos or stories, remove the app, or erase its storage. Deleting an entry removes its local app copy; it does not remove originals in Photos or copies you have exported or shared. Delete those copies separately in the relevant service.

Device or operating-system backups may include app data according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. Frameago does not operate its own backup or cloud-sync service. Uninstalling can permanently remove your journal. Image and video exports are not a complete, restorable journal backup.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue, not for marketing. Correspondence is handled through Gmail under Google’s applicable service and privacy terms. We retain it only as needed to resolve and follow up on the request or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, or tracking pixels. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting, as explained in GitHub’s privacy statement. Website requests are separate from the journal stored in the app.

Children

Frameago is a general-purpose photo journal and is not specifically directed at children. The app does not collect personal information from users, including children. If a child sends personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change. Any future feature that changes how personal data is handled will be described here.
