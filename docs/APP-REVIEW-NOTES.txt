Frameago is a free, offline photo journal for iPhone and iPad. No login, subscription, in-app purchase, server connection, or special hardware is required.

Suggested review path:
1. On the home screen, tap "Explore a sample story" to open "A little greener". The bundled photos show different plants and are labelled as samples for demonstration.
2. Open "Compare photos" and drag the before/after slider. Select the two moments to compare.
3. Export a still image or create a six-second 1080 x 1920 MP4. Keep the app open while the video is prepared. Preview it, save it to Photos, or open the system share sheet. Sharing never posts automatically.
4. Return home and choose Library to import a photo, or Take photo on a device with a camera. The first photo starts a story; add another moment from that story. Dates, notes, titles, and categories can be edited.
5. Open About using the information button in the header to find the privacy policy and support links.

Camera access is requested only when taking a photo. Photos add-only permission is requested when saving an exported video. No microphone permission is requested.

Photos, notes, and journal metadata remain in local app storage. There are no user accounts, analytics, advertisements, cloud sync, or automatic uploads. Removing the app may remove the journal; exported media is not a complete restorable backup. The app and public support page explain this limitation.
